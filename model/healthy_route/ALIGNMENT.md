## Trajectory mapping, endpoints and offsets

The [fictional request](../tests/fixtures/canopy/health_inference.json) supplies 13 positions from `[0,0,0]` to `[120,0,0]`, at 10 m increments along east. There is no road mapping, building input, traffic input, snapping or position offset. It is a local test trajectory, not a London route. Custom requests do not automatically inherit geographic validity from an existing Scenario.

For a private result, the exact endpoint node IDs are `parameters.origin` and `parameters.goal` in its retained `provenance/request.json`. Resolve them against that result's exact bundled `pedestrian/generated/network.json`, not a current road graph. `route.json.routes[1].edge_keys` selects the ordered directed network edges; `timeline[].points` and `distance` preserve their geometry. Route start/end are the first/last selected timeline positions. Those private result values are not published in this document.

[Network generation](../src/healthy_route/build_network.py) nodes visible mapped footway/path/pedestrian/steps at the same level, honors covered-segment breaks, preserves parallel paths, and joins bridge entrances only at exact mapped endpoints with height agreement within 0.1 m. This is geometric connectivity, not surveyed accessibility. There is no arbitrary horizontal translation added by the health adapter. Native road/deck geometry affects the network, but no building GLB is consumed by either health Method.

[Private export](../src/canopy/models/healthy_route.py) preserves every route vertex, wait boundary and breathing-update timestamp. Moving positions interpolate along the event's distance axis; waits hold position. The [old inactive UI](../../visualizer/src/visualization/healthy-route/canopy-pedestrian.mjs) can flatten to zero when terrain is off and adds presentation offsets (walker +0.15 m, road overlay +0.3 m, sample points +0.5 m, flags +0.6 m). These are display offsets, absent from scientific trajectory coordinates and unsuitable as inferred city alignment metadata.

## Time semantics and coverage

Both exporters produce explicit relative seconds without a UTC epoch. `created_at` is execution time, not observation time. See [public inference/export](../src/canopy/models/health_inference.py) and [private export](../src/canopy/models/healthy_route.py). The Scenario profile uses relative alignment; equal second values do not establish a simultaneous real event.

- **Fictional example:** samples are 0–3600 s at 300 s spacing. N exposure rows represent N five-minute intervals; initial breathing is at zero and each prediction is available at the next boundary. Exposure row i is displayed from 300i until the next boundary; the last row is held at the final sample. Irradiance and positions have N+1 supplied boundary samples. Series use step sampling, trajectory uses linear sampling. Environmental inputs are caller-supplied conditional values, not live measurements or separately predicted weather.
- **Private route:** elapsed zero is route departure; environment lookup uses `departure_s + elapsed`. The source profile is sampled at event endpoints and subdivisions no longer than about 2 s, then the export additionally retains exact vertices and breathing updates. Read the actual manifest sample array: it is not uniformly sampled. Physiology updates on a 300 s recurrent clock whose phase comes from the private initialization; it need not first update at elapsed 300 s. Its historical clock and state are independent of the city clock and remain private.
- **Traffic:** release metadata records `t0=31800` (08:50), `dt=1`, 600 frames; actual frame timestamps span 31800–32399 s. The scenario repeats a 600 s period. This is retained SUMO simulation, not measured traffic and not a UTC timestamp. Pedestrian signals are independent model assumptions, not observed controllers: red 40 s, green 10 s, clearance derived from crossing length, origin 0 in the generated network. Vehicle-gap admission does not make these signals control the recorded cars.
- **Environment:** temperature has 11 samples, 0–600 s at 60 s spacing (256×256, °C); tracer has 21 samples, 0–600 s at 30 s spacing (1024×1024, arbitrary units). The model samples a square `[-4096,4096)` XY grid, with row north/column east and cell size 8192/width metres. Time interpolation, modulo replay and a smooth blend to the first frame over the last 60 s are explicit implementation choices. The tracer-to-PM2.5 proxy is uncalibrated.
- **Solar:** summer has 99 samples at 600 s spacing, 17400–76200 s; winter has 47, 29400–57000 s, both 1024×1024 W/m². Lookup uses `31800 + departure_s + elapsed` on the selected scenario day, without replay. No UTC conversion is established by that arithmetic. Outside supplied solar coverage, the current implementation returns zero; this is an assumed extension, not an observed nighttime value.

Exact sources: [environment sampling](../src/healthy_route/environment.py), [traffic gap logic](../src/healthy_route/traffic_gaps.py), [signal generator](../src/healthy_route/build_network.py), [physiology timing](../src/healthy_route/corridor_breathing.py), [extended replay profile](../../scenario/imperial_corridor_8km/experiments/healthy_route_extended_replay/profile.json). The environment metadata explicitly says that aligning first traffic frame, field zero and 08:50 is **not a jointly observed event**. The 13-hour maximum is experiment coverage, not 13 hours of new environmental recordings.

## Missing values and display policy

The public Method rejects nonfinite feature/position data and nonphysical predictions; the finite fictional fixture needs no missing-data interpolation. Private trajectory export requires a finite, contiguous timeline; it does not bridge missing events. The Viewer hides unavailable samples outside the retained time axis. Environmental series with missing/nonfinite values are omitted from the private scientific export; the full `route.json` retains their missing values.

Before route evaluation, the historical environment implementation fills masked/invalid grid cells from the nearest valid grid cell and records donor mappings. Missing route samples outside the grid can remain null; physiology can substitute training means with an explicit routing penalty. Filled cells are **extrapolated**, not measured valid cells. Original source masks remain in the bundle. The solar zero extension noted above also requires an explicit assumption label; it must not be relabelled as observed coverage. A future city widget must preserve availability and provenance and must not silently replace missing samples with zeros or draw a continuous path across absent positions.
